How to Play Five Crowns

🎯 Objective

Have the lowest score after all 11 rounds. Form your entire hand into books and/or runs to go out and score zero!

🃏 The Deck

Two 58-card decks combined. Five suits: ★ Stars, ♥ Hearts, ♣ Clubs, ♠ Spades, ♦ Diamonds. Cards 3–10, Jack, Queen, King, plus 3 Jokers per deck.

🔄 Rounds & Wilds

11 rounds. Round 1 = 3 cards each. One more card each round. The wild card rotates (Round 1: 3s wild … Round 11: Kings wild). Jokers always wild.

📚 Books & Runs

Book: 3+ same rank (any suit). Run: 3+ consecutive same suit. Wilds substitute for any card.

▶️ Your Turn

1. Draw from the deck or take the top discard.
2. Rearrange your hand (drag).
3. Tap a card to select it, then tap the Discard pile to end your turn.

🚪 Going Out

Tap GO OUT, assign all cards into meld groups (books/runs) and mark one card to discard. Submit to go out with a score of 0. Everyone else gets one final draw+discard turn.

📋 Final Cards Panel

On your final turn, draw a card then discard one. Your remaining cards appear in the right-side Final panel. Place them in sets to reduce your score — leftover unmelded cards count as points.

💯 Scoring

Unmelded cards count against you: number cards = face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13, Jokers=50, Round wilds=20. Lowest total after 11 rounds wins!

How to Play Five Crowns

🎯 Objective

Have the lowest score after all 11 rounds. Form your entire hand into books and/or runs to go out and score zero!

🃏 The Deck

Two 58-card decks combined. Five suits: ★ Stars, ♥ Hearts, ♣ Clubs, ♠ Spades, ♦ Diamonds. Cards 3–10, Jack (11), Queen (12), King (13), plus 3 Jokers per deck.

🔄 Rounds & Wild Cards

11 rounds total. Round 1 starts with 3 cards each, and each round you get one more card. The wild card rotates every round — in Round 1, 3s are wild; Round 2, 4s are wild… up to Kings in Round 11. Jokers are always wild.

📚 Books & Runs

Book: 3 or more cards of the same value (any suits). Example: 7★ 7♥ 7♣
Run: 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit. Example: 5♥ 6♥ 7♥
Wild cards (Jokers + round wilds) can substitute for any card.

▶️ Your Turn

1. Draw the top card from the draw pile or take the top discard.
2. Rearrange your hand by dragging cards.
3. Select a card then tap the discard pile to end your turn.

🚪 Going Out

When your entire hand can form books/runs (with one card left to discard), click GO OUT. Your melds are shown on the table so everyone can see. All other players get one final turn.

💯 Scoring

Cards left in hand count against you: Number cards = face value, Jacks = 11, Queens = 12, Kings = 13, Jokers = 50, Wild cards for that round = 20. Lowest total after 11 rounds wins!
