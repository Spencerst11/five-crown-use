How to Play Five Crowns

🎯 Objective

Have the lowest score after all 11 rounds. Form your entire hand into books and/or runs to go out and score zero!

🃏 The Deck

Two 58-card decks combined. Five suits: ★ Stars, ♥ Hearts, ♣ Clubs, ♠ Spades, ♦ Diamonds. Cards 3–10, Jack (11), Queen (12), King (13), plus 3 Jokers per deck.

🔄 Rounds & Wild Cards

11 rounds total. Round 1 starts with 3 cards each, and each round you get one more card. The wild card rotates every round — in Round 1, 3s are wild; Round 2, 4s are wild… up to Kings in Round 11. Jokers are always wild.

📚 Books & Runs

Book: 3 or more cards of the same value (any suits). Example: 7★ 7♥ 7♣
Run: 3 or more consecutive cards in the same suit. Example: 5♥ 6♥ 7♥
Wild cards (Jokers + round wilds) can substitute for any card.

▶️ Your Turn

1. Draw the top card from the draw pile or take the top discard.
2. Rearrange your hand to form books/runs.
3. Discard one card to end your turn.

🚪 Going Out

When your entire hand can form books/runs (with one card left to discard), click GO OUT. All other players get one final turn, then everyone scores their leftover cards.

💯 Scoring

Cards left in hand count against you: Number cards = face value, Jacks = 11, Queens = 12, Kings = 13, Wild cards (current round) = 20, Jokers = 50. Lowest total after 11 rounds wins!

🃏 Card Highlighting in This Game

Wild cards glow gold. Possible books pulse green. Click cards to select them, then click the discard pile to discard. Use "GO OUT" when your hand is fully arranged.
